# Privacy Policy – StudyCraft

Last updated: November 2026 · [Deutsche Fassung](datenschutz)

**In short: StudyCraft does not collect, store or transmit personal data to the developer or to third parties.
The app's only network connection is used to fetch ready-made decks in the “Entdecken” (Discover) tab.**

## What data does the app process?

StudyCraft works with files in a folder you choose yourself (for example in iCloud Drive): your Markdown cards, images in that folder
and your study progress (due dates and statistics), which is stored in the subfolder “_StudyCraft”. This data stays on your device
and in your own iCloud Drive storage. Syncing is handled by Apple (iCloud Drive) under Apple's privacy terms. The developer has no access to it.

Settings (for example appearance, study order, Ultra Dopamine Mode) and the state of unfinished study sessions are stored locally on
your device only.

## Discover: fetching decks

When you open the “Entdecken” tab, the app downloads a list of ready-made decks with preview images from the project's public
repository on GitHub. When you add a deck, its files (cards as Markdown text and images) are copied from there to your device.

- For these requests **GitHub** (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company) processes the technical connection data that any web request
  involves, such as your IP address, the time and the requested file. See
  [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- The developer of StudyCraft receives **no** data about you, your devices or your study behavior. The app does not send identifiers,
  accounts or anything about your cards.
- The most recently loaded deck list is cached on your device so it can be shown offline.

If you never open the “Entdecken” tab, the app makes no internet connection.

## What the app does not do

- no accounts or sign-in
- no analytics or tracking, no advertising, no cookies
- no sharing of data with third parties
- no connection to servers operated by the developer

## Photos

If you choose “insert image” while editing a card, iOS hands the app only the photo you picked. The app gets no access to your photo
library. The image is copied, scaled down, into your study folder.

## Sound and haptics

The optional Ultra Dopamine Mode plays sounds and vibrations. Both are generated on the device; nothing is recorded and no microphone is used.

## Deleting your data

You can delete cards, decks and the “_StudyCraft” folder at any time in the Files app or on a Mac. Deleting the app removes only the
local settings; your files in the study folder remain.

## Children

StudyCraft is suitable for all ages and collects personal data from no one.

## Your rights

Because the developer stores no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on the developer's side. You can
exercise your rights against GitHub and Apple under their privacy terms, and you can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

## Changes

If the app changes, this policy will be updated. The date above shows the current version.
